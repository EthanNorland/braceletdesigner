## Inspiration & Design Precedents

### 1. Modular Charm Bracelets (Trollbeads & Pandora)
- **Concept Borrowed:** The tactile pleasure of stringing and arranging interchangeable glass, stone, and metal tokens onto a loop.
- **How Ours Differs:** Fully open and creative rather than a commercial sales funnel; provides custom bead sizing, wrist fit calculation, and itemized materials spec export.

### 2. Physical Tabletop Creative Software (Kai's Power Tools / KPT Goo)
- **Author:** Kai Krause / MetaCreations (1990s)
- **Concept Borrowed:** Tangible, tactile direct manipulation of physical-feeling digital materials.
