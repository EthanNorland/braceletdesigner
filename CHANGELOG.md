## [1.9.39] - 2026-10-27

### Fixed - Open Spot Bead Placement & Barrel Bead Flush Contact
- **Open Spot Placement on Add & Duplicate ([`index.html`](file:///Users/ethannorland/braceletdesigner/index.html)):**
  - Resolved the bug where newly added beads and duplicated beads (`D`) were placed directly on top of existing beads, causing overlapping collisions.
  - Implemented `findOpenSpotForBead(newBeadSpec, preferredBead)` which scans all intervals along the cord loop (before first bead, between adjacent beads, and after the last bead) to locate legitimate empty spaces with zero overlap.
  - When duplicating a bead, checks for open space immediately adjacent to the selected bead; if flanked, places the duplicate in the open space at the end of the strand or largest open gap.
  - When adding a bead, places it in the open space at the end of the strand (or first open gap).
  - If the bracelet is completely full, prevents spawning overlapping beads and triggers an informative toast notice: *"Bracelet base is full! Increase wrist size or remove beads to add more."*
- **Barrel Bead Flush Lining Up Calibration:**
  - Resolved the visible empty air gap (~1.5mm on each side) between barrel beads and adjacent beads.
  - Diagnosed that `createHollowBarrelGeometry` models barrel beads with length $h = \text{beadRadius} \times 1.5$ ($0.75 \times \text{sizeMm}$), but `getBeadThicknessMm` was returning nominal outer diameter $s$ ($1.0 \times \text{sizeMm}$), creating a 3mm phantom separation chord.
  - Calibrated `getBeadHalfThicknessUnits` and `getBeadThicknessMm` to exact mesh geometry formulas across all shapes:
    - Barrel beads: $0.75 \times \text{sizeMm}$ ($12\text{mm} = 9\text{mm}$ cord length)
    - Barrel / carved spacers: $0.65 \times \text{sizeMm}$ ($8\text{mm} = 5.2\text{mm}$ cord length)
    - Spheres / faceted crystals: $0.95 \times \text{sizeMm}$ ($10\text{mm} = 10\text{mm}$)
    - Monogram cubes: $0.825 \times \text{sizeMm}$ ($10\text{mm} = 8\text{mm}$)
    - Star beads: $0.405 \times \text{sizeMm}$ ($10\text{mm} = 4\text{mm}$)
    - Rondelle spacers / beads: $0.52 \times \text{sizeMm}$ ($6\text{mm} = 3\text{mm}$, $11\text{mm} = 6\text{mm}$)
  - Barrel beads now touch flush against spacers and beads on either side with exact $0.000\text{mm}$ air gap.
- **PWA & Documentation Sync ([`sw.js`](file:///Users/ethannorland/braceletdesigner/sw.js)):**
  - Bumped version to `v1.9.39` across `sw.js`, `index.html`, `landing.html`, `welcome.html`, `portfolio.html`, `README.md`, `CHANGELOG.md`, and `HANDOFF.md`.

## [1.9.38] - 2026-10-27

### Fixed - Flush Bead Contact & Unified Physical Thickness Accumulator
- **True Physical Cord Thickness Calibration ([`index.html`](file:///Users/ethannorland/braceletdesigner/index.html)):**
  - Resolved the empty room issue where the live millimeter accumulator reached full bracelet length (`totalUsedMm == doc.wristMm`) while visible cord space remained unfilled, and beads exhibited floating gaps between them.
  - Implemented `getBeadThicknessMm(shape, sizeMm, isSpacer)` and `getBeadHalfThicknessUnits()` calibrated directly to the 3D meshes along the cord axis (Z):
    - Rondelle spacer / bead: $0.55 \times \text{sizeMm}$ (e.g. 6mm spacer $= 3\text{mm}$, 11mm rondelle $= 6\text{mm}$)
    - Star bead: $0.45 \times \text{sizeMm}$ (e.g. 10mm star $= 5\text{mm}$)
    - Carved spacer: $0.70 \times \text{sizeMm}$ (e.g. 8mm carved $= 6\text{mm}$)
    - Spacer barrel: $0.75 \times \text{sizeMm}$ (e.g. 8mm spacer barrel $= 6\text{mm}$; 12mm barrel bead $= 12\text{mm}$)
    - Sphere, faceted, cube: $1.00 \times \text{sizeMm}$ (e.g. 10mm bead $= 10\text{mm}$)
- **Flush Bead Contact Without Gaps or Penetration:**
  - Calibrated contact chord in `getBeadContactDelta(b1, b2, radius)` to $t_1 + t_2$ (half-thicknesses along cord), placing beads touching directly next to each other flush with hairline contact ($0\text{mm}$ air gap).
  - Calibrated `getClaspContactDelta(bead, radius)` to $t_{\text{bead}}$, seating the first and last beads directly adjacent to clasp ferrules.
  - Updated `addBead()`, `layoutTemplateAngles()`, and startup preset in `STARTUP_DOC` and [`reference/Braceletuponappstartup.bracelet`](file:///Users/ethannorland/braceletdesigner/reference/Braceletuponappstartup.bracelet) with exact flush angles.
- **Harmonized Fit Calculation (`calculateFit`):**
  - Updated `calculateFit()` accumulator to sum true physical cord thickness `getBeadThicknessMm(...)` rather than nominal outer diameter.
  - Now every millimeter counted on the accumulator corresponds to an exact physical millimeter occupied along the cord: when `totalUsedMm == doc.wristMm`, beads span 100% of the cord loop from ferrule to ferrule with zero remaining room.
- **Dynamic Wrist Sizing & Overlap Resolution:**
  - Implemented `reflowBeadsForWristSize(beads, oldWristMm, newWristMm)` preserving physical cord positions and flush bead contact when wrist size slider is moved.
  - Implemented `resolveBeadOverlaps(beads, wristMm)` ensuring beads dynamically push and accommodate neighbors when bead size or shape is edited in Properties Inspector.

  - Dynamically positioned new beads in `addBead()` immediately adjacent to the previous bead or clasp ferrule.
  - Updated `layoutTemplateAngles()` to position template strands flush starting adjacent to the clasp ferrule.
  - Updated startup preset angles in `STARTUP_DOC` and [`reference/Braceletuponappstartup.bracelet`](file:///Users/ethannorland/braceletdesigner/reference/Braceletuponappstartup.bracelet) to match the calibrated scale.
