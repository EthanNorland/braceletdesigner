Help & Quick Guide

Welcome to Bracelet Designer, your tactile 3D jewelry workshop.

Rotate & Inspect: Click and drag in the 3D viewport to spin the bracelet under studio lighting.
Add Elements: Select beads, spacers, and clasps from the left palette to add them to your strand.
Tailor Fit & Materials: Adjust wrist circumference and bead diameters in the right properties panel.
