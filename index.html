Help & Core Guide

    
Welcome to Bracelet Designer, your tactile 3D jewelry workshop.

    

      
    

    
    

      

        Starter Templates & Presets: Click New in the header to browse curated atelier templates (The Signature Atelier, Minimalist 18K Heirloom, Bohemian Earth, Chakra Spectrum, Monogram Keepsake) or start with a clean cord.
      

      

        Preview on Wrist: Toggle Wrist View in the header to drape your bracelet onto a realistic anatomical human arm and posed hand with 4 skin tones.
      

      

        Tactile Audio Engine: Toggle Sound in the header for real-time procedural Web Audio synthesis that resonates according to bead material (glass, quartz, metal, stone, wood), sliding collisions, and clasp latching.
      

      

        Slide & Arrange: Click directly on any bead in 3D and drag to slide it along the bracelet curve.
      

      

        String New Elements: Click beads, spacers, or clasps in the left palette to add them to your strand.
      

      

        Customize Material & Size: Select any bead to change its millimeter diameter, color, shape, or material (Glass, Metal, Stone, Wood).
      

      

        Cube Inscriptions: Select any Cube bead to engrave a custom letter (A-Z), number (0-9), or symbol (Heart, Cross, Smiley, Peace, Dash).
      

      

        Open & Save: Download your design as a .bracelet project file (Cmd+S) or drag any saved file directly onto the 3D stage (Cmd+O) to reopen it.
      

      

        Continuous Autosave: Your workspace automatically persists in browser storage so you can close or reload anytime without losing your work.
      

      

        Duplicate & Delete: Use the action bar or press D to duplicate and Delete to remove beads.
      

      

        Materials Spec Sheet: View an itemized recipe with dimensions, material finishes, and quantities for jeweler or craft assembly, plus CSV export.
      

      

        Export High-Res PNG: Capture a crisp studio render of your bracelet without UI overlays.

Bracelet DESIGNER

    
"Bracelet Designer exists because everyone should be able to test their creative instincts and feel like they are manipulating physical materials on a tabletop."

    

      Version: 1.9.23

      Created by Ethan Norland with Antigravity pair programming.

      Released under the MIT License. 3D rendering powered by Three.js.

      100% Client-side atelier: your designs persist locally in IndexedDB; nothing is ever uploaded.
