Guiding Principles

Craftsmanship Over Bloat

Software is most rewarding when it behaves like a set of finely tuned physical tools on a real workshop bench.

✦ Tactile Physicality

Digital objects shouldn't feel flat or lifeless. When dragging a bead along a cord, physical collision feedback, acoustic material resonance, and realistic specular luster make digital manipulation deeply satisfying.

✦ Privacy & Autonomy

Users shouldn't have to create accounts, accept tracking cookies, or depend on remote clouds just to explore a creative idea. All designs persist locally in the browser, completely private.

✦ Zero-Dependency Rigor

Bypassing massive framework overhead results in lightning-fast initial load times (<50ms), pristine 60fps performance on mobile, and codebases that endure for years without dependency breakage.
